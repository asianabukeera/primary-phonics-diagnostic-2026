Reading Stages

Key Findings

  • Many learners identified letter names but struggled to produce sounds.
  • Word blending remains a challenge.
  • Paragraph reading is a major bottleneck in P1 and P2.
  • Consonant blends remain difficult for many learners.
  • Reading comprehension is weaker than decoding for some learners.

Recommendations

  1. Strengthen phonemic awareness instruction.
  2. Increase blending practice.
  3. Provide targeted support for struggling readers.
  4. Strengthen teaching of consonant blends.
  5. Integrate comprehension activities into daily lessons.
