Primary Phonics Diagnostic Assessment July 2026 Assessment Report 420 Learners Assessed K-P4 Grades Covered 5 Reading Stages Multi Schools Assessed Assessment Overview The Primary Phonics Diagnostic Assessment evaluated learner performance across five stages of reading development: Sound Recognition Word Reading Paragraph Reading Story Reading Reading Comprehension Key Findings Many learners identified letter names but struggled to produce the correct sounds. Word blending remains a challenge for many learners. Paragraph reading is a major bottleneck in Primary 1 and Primary 2. Consonant blends such as ch, sh, th, bl, br and str remain difficult for many learners. Several learners could decode text but struggled with comprehension. Main Reading Challenges Letter Names vs Sounds Many learners appear to know letter names but struggle to produce the corresponding sound. Blending Skills Learners often struggle to combine sounds into complete words. Paragraph Reading The transition from reading isolated words to connected text remains difficult. Consonant Blends Complex blends remain a challenge for many learners in P3 and P4. Comprehension Some learners can read stories accurately but cannot answer comprehension questions. Recommendations Strengthen phonemic awareness instruction in Kindergarten and Primary 1. Increase guided blending and decoding activities. Provide targeted support for struggling readers. Strengthen teaching of consonant blends and advanced phonics patterns. Integrate comprehension activities into daily reading lessons. Conduct regular phonics diagnostic assessments to monitor learner progress. Use diagnostic data to identify learners requiring additional support. Primary Phonics Diagnostic Assessment Report | 2026
